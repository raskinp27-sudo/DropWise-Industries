Background Research & Problem Definition

Building the case for a safer way to receive: why porch piracy matters, who it hurts, and what people are asking for.

Problem Definition

Porch piracy has intensified with the growth of online shopping, leaving unattended packages exposed to theft at the doorstep. Existing options—cameras, lockers, and smart locks—do not consistently provide a reliable, secure, and user-friendly way to prevent that loss.

Relevant Statistics

Visual evidence of scale and cost—each figure reinforces why a dedicated secure receiving solution is needed.

Target Market Demographics

Those most affected include suburban and urban homeowners who receive frequent deliveries, renters in multi-unit buildings with shared or exposed entryways, heavy online shoppers (families, remote workers, and small-business owners receiving inventory at home), and anyone relying on time-sensitive goods where a stolen box is more than an inconvenience.

Potential User Survey Results

Informed by typical user research on package security, respondents would likely report high concern about theft, frustration with missed or stolen deliveries, and strong interest in solutions that physically protect packages without complicated steps for carriers. Many would prioritize ease of use, visibility or alerts when something goes wrong, and compatibility with both houses and apartments—setting clear requirements for what a successful product must deliver.

Why Common Approaches Fall Short

  • Cameras (like video doorbells) only record theft and do not prevent it
  • Lockers are often limited to apartments and have size restrictions
  • Smart locks can be expensive and unreliable without package detection
